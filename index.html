How the rules are read
  1. Only USDT pairs priced under the max price, using each pair's price at scan time.
  2. Support: a bearish Daily candle followed by a bullish one. Support is the bearish candle's close. Touches count from the candle after the bullish one.
  3. Rejection: the previous closed Daily candle is the first to reach the support (low at or under it) and closes above it.
  4. Resistance: an H4 bullish candle followed by a bearish one, at the bullish close, above the support, completed before the touch and never closed above before it. The lowest one is used.
  5. Break: an H4 close above the resistance, checked from the H4 candle that first touched the support. A break inside that Daily candle is flagged as an alert at the daily reset.
  6. Exception: if a later H4 candle sweeps the first touch's low and closes above the support, the highest unbroken resistance formed after the first touch replaces the original, and an H4 close above it triggers.
  7. Supports are searched in the last 365 Daily candles and resistances in the last 400 H4 candles.
